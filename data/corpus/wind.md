# Wind Power

Wind turbines convert the kinetic energy of moving air into electricity. As
wind passes over the turbine's blades, it causes them to rotate, spinning a
shaft connected to a generator. The generator uses electromagnetic induction
to produce electricity. Wind is a renewable resource, and modern turbines can
operate both onshore and offshore. Output varies with wind speed, so wind farms
are often paired with storage or other sources to provide steady power.
