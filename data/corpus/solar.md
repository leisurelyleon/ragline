# Solar Power

Solar panels convert sunlight directly into electricity using photovoltaic
cells made primarily of silicon. When photons strike the cell, they knock
electrons loose, and the resulting flow of electrons is captured as direct
current. An inverter then converts that direct current into the alternating
current used by homes and the electrical grid. Solar power is renewable,
produces no emissions during operation, and scales from small rooftop arrays
to large utility installations.
