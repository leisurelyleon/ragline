# Hydroelectric Power

Hydroelectric dams convert the energy of falling or flowing water into
electricity. Water held behind a dam is released through turbines, and the
force of the moving water spins them to drive a generator. Hydroelectric power
is renewable and dispatchable, meaning output can be adjusted to meet demand.
It currently provides a large share of the world's renewable electricity,
though building dams can significantly alter local river ecosystems.
